Coronavirus (COVID-19)

Stay informed. Stay safe. Learn about the disease, its symptoms, and how to protect yourself and others.

What is Coronavirus?

Coronavirus disease (COVID-19) is an infectious disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus. Most people infected will experience mild to moderate respiratory illness and recover without requiring special treatment. However, older people and those with underlying conditions such as heart disease, diabetes, or cancer are more likely to develop serious illness.

Symptoms

COVID-19 affects people differently. Symptoms usually appear 2–14 days after exposure.

🌡️ Fever or chills
😴 Fatigue or tiredness
🤧 Dry cough
💨 Shortness of breath
👃 Loss of smell or taste
🦴 Body aches and pains
🤒 Sore throat
🤢 Nausea or diarrhoea (rare)
⚠️ Seek medical help immediately if you have difficulty breathing, chest pain, or confusion. These can be signs of severe illness.

How COVID-19 Spreads

The virus spreads mainly through contact with an infected person. Here are the main ways:

💧 Droplets When an infected person coughs, sneezes, or talks, droplets carry the virus.
🌬️ Airborne Small particles can stay in the air in poorly ventilated indoor spaces.
🤝 Close Contact Being near an infected person increases your chances of breathing in the virus.

How to Prevent COVID-19

Simple everyday actions can significantly reduce the spread of the virus:

  • Wash your hands regularly with soap and water for at least 20 seconds.
  • Use an alcohol-based hand sanitiser when soap is not available.
  • Maintain at least 1 metre distance from people who are coughing or sneezing.
  • Avoid touching your eyes, nose, and mouth with unwashed hands.
  • Wear a mask properly in crowded or indoor places.
  • Stay home and isolate if you feel unwell or have symptoms.
  • Cover your mouth and nose with your elbow when you cough or sneeze.
  • Keep rooms well-ventilated by opening windows when possible.
